FNL Playbook — Version 1
6-7 Year Old Friday Night Lights Flag Football  •  One Formation. Three Plays.

Color = Where

BLUE = run at the blue cone
GOLD = run at the gold cone

Action = What

SWEEP — wide & fast
GHOST — fake, then give it back
BUBBLE — short catch, then run
Cones flip every time we change ends, so "BLUE" always means the same thing to a 6-year-old: run at the cone you can see.
Diagram key: every diagram in this book is drawn with downfield = up the page (toward the opponent's end zone). Backs, QB and Center start below the line of scrimmage; any real run path must end pointing toward the top of the diagram.

Formation: STAR

Same 6 spots, every single play. QB under center — hand-to-hand snap, never shotgun.
#SpotWhere you find it (kid words)
1CENTER (C)Stand on the ball.
2QBDirectly behind the Center, hands under him, toes almost touching his heels.
3BLUE BACK (BB)Beside the QB, one big step toward the blue cone.
4GOLD BACK (GB)Beside the QB, one big step toward the gold cone.
5BLUE WIDE (BW)On the blue sideline, even with the ball.
6GOLD WIDE (GW)On the gold sideline, even with the ball.

Why under center, not shotgun

The Center hikes the ball up into the QB's hands, hand-to-hand — the ball never flies through the air, so a dropped snap is nearly eliminated. Rushers must start 7 yards off the line, so the QB has time even with his back briefly to the defense. Ref §6, §9. Confirm hand-to-hand exchange legality with the league before the first game (Ref §16 item 21).

BLUE SWEEP

Type: RUN — our base play. Get wide first, then go north.
Ball carrier's real path Fake / decoy
CENTER: Hike the ball up into the QB's hands, then run five steps straight ahead and look for the ball.
QB: Feel the ball, open your feet toward the blue cone, and put the ball in the Blue Back's belly on step 2.
BLUE BACK: Take the ball and run wide toward the blue cone, then turn upfield toward the end zone.
GOLD BACK: Run hard the other way toward the gold cone so they chase you.
BLUE WIDE: Count 5 steps straight upfield, then break in toward the middle, flat across the QB's vision.
GOLD WIDE: Count 5 steps straight upfield, then break in toward the middle, flat across the QB's vision.

When to call it

First play of a series, any time we need normal yards. Not in a no-run zone (5 yards off each end zone / 5 yards either side of midfield) — call BLUE BUBBLE there instead.

Coaching point

"Get wide FIRST, then go north." To the QB: "Feel it, then turn." Hands away from your flags — no flag guarding. Wides: count your own 5 steps, then break in — don't drift back toward the line of scrimmage.

GOLD GHOST

Type: RUN with a fake — BLUE SWEEP's twin. Only works after we've shown BLUE SWEEP.
Ball carrier's real path Fake / decoy
CENTER: Hike the ball up into the QB's hands, then run five steps ahead and look for the ball.
QB: Secure the snap, open to the blue cone and fake to the Blue Back on step 2, then spin back and hand it for real to the Gold Back.
BLUE BACK: Run the same path as BLUE SWEEP with empty hands and pretend you have the ball.
GOLD BACK: Take one small step toward the blue cone, count one beat, then take the handoff and turn upfield toward the gold cone.
BLUE WIDE: Count 5 steps straight upfield, then break in toward the middle, flat across the QB's vision.
GOLD WIDE: Count 5 steps straight upfield, then break in toward the middle, flat across the QB's vision.

When to call it

After BLUE SWEEP has worked once or twice and the defense is flying to the blue side. Great on second down when we need a chunk. Not in a no-run zone.

Coaching point

"The fake runs just as hard as the ball." To the QB: "Ball, turn, fake, turn, give." Rehearse the mesh at half speed a lot before calling it in a game. Wides: count your own 5 steps, then break in — no drifting back toward the line of scrimmage.

BLUE BUBBLE

Type: PASS — short, easy. Receiver already faces the QB before the ball comes.
Ball carrier's real path Pre-snap motion Pass path
CENTER: Hike the ball up into the QB's hands, then run five steps ahead and turn around.
QB: Secure the snap, step straight back two steps, then toss the ball soft and low to the Blue Back right away.
BLUE BACK: Walk toward the blue cone before the snap, then stop, turn around, and face the QB with hands up.
GOLD BACK: Stay behind the QB and face him — if the ball comes to you, catch it and run.
BLUE WIDE: Count 5 steps straight upfield, then break in toward the middle, flat across the QB's vision, to pull the cornerback away from the edge.
GOLD WIDE: Count 5 steps straight upfield, then break in toward the middle, flat across the QB's vision, to pull the cornerback away from the edge.

When to call it

Inside a no-run zone (near either end zone or midfield) — those spots must be pass plays. Also any time we need a first down and the defense has crowded the run.

Coaching point

"Turn around EARLY, hands up, eyes on the ball." To the QB: "Ball, back-back, throw." Yards come after the catch, not in the air. Wides: count your own 5 steps, then break in flat — no drifting back toward the line of scrimmage.
