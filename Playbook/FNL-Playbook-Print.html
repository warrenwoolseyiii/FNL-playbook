FNL Playbook — Version 1
6-7 Year Old Friday Night Lights Flag Football  •  One Formation. Five Plays. Two Base Defenses — FORT (Man) & FENCE (2-3-1 Zone), starting with FORT.

Color = Where (fixed to the field)

BLUE = offense's RIGHT side
ORANGE = offense's LEFT side

Action = What

SWEEP — wide & fast
GHOST — fake, then give it back
BUBBLE — short catch, then run
DART — fake the run, throw it downfield
FLYER — massive misdirection, hardest play in the book
BLUE = RIGHT, ORANGE = LEFT — always, from the offense's point of view facing downfield. Colors no longer flip with direction. #1 confusion risk at this age: use a wristband on each side, or blue/orange cones on the correct sidelines every game.
Diagram key: every diagram in this book is drawn with downfield = up the page (toward the opponent's end zone). Backs, QB and Center start below the line of scrimmage; any real run path must end pointing toward the top of the diagram.

Formation: STAR

Same 6 spots, every single play. QB under center — hand-to-hand snap, never shotgun.
#SpotWhere you find it (kid words)
1CENTER (C)Stand on the ball.
2QBDirectly behind the Center, hands under him, toes almost touching his heels.
3BLUE BACK (BB)Directly behind the QB, one big step back, one small step toward the blue cone — tight split-back look.
4ORANGE BACK (OB)Directly behind the QB, one big step back, one small step toward the orange cone — tight split-back look.
5BLUE WIDE (BW)On the blue sideline, even with the ball.
6ORANGE WIDE (OW)On the orange sideline, even with the ball.

BLUE SWEEP

Type: RUN — our base play. Get wide first, then go north.
Ball carrier's real path Fake / decoy
CENTER: Hike the ball up into the QB's hands, then run five steps straight ahead and look for the ball.
QB: Feel the ball, open your feet toward the blue cone, and put the ball in the Blue Back's belly on your first step.
BLUE BACK: Take the ball and run wide toward the blue cone, then turn upfield.
ORANGE BACK: Run hard the other way toward the orange cone so they chase you.
BLUE WIDE: Count 5 steps straight upfield, then break in toward the middle.
ORANGE WIDE: Count 5 steps straight upfield, then break in toward the middle.

ORANGE GHOST

Type: RUN with a fake — BLUE SWEEP's twin. Only works after we've shown BLUE SWEEP.
Ball carrier's real path Fake / decoy
CENTER: Hike the ball up into the QB's hands, then run five steps ahead and look for the ball.
QB: Fake to the Blue Back on step 1, then spin back and hand it for real to the Orange Back on step 2.
BLUE BACK: Run the same path as BLUE SWEEP with empty hands and pretend you have the ball.
ORANGE BACK: Barely move on the fake, then take the handoff and turn upfield toward the orange cone.
BLUE WIDE: Count 5 steps straight upfield, then break in toward the middle.
ORANGE WIDE: Count 5 steps straight upfield, then break in toward the middle.

BLUE BUBBLE

Type: PASS — short, easy. Receiver already faces the QB before the ball comes.
Ball carrier's real path Pre-snap motion Pass path
CENTER: Hike the ball to the QB, then run five steps ahead and turn around.
QB: Step straight back two steps, then toss the ball soft and low to the Blue Back.
BLUE BACK: Walk toward the blue cone before the snap, then stop, turn, and face the QB with hands up.
ORANGE BACK: Stay tight behind the QB and face him — safety valve if the ball comes to you.
BLUE WIDE: Count 5 steps upfield, then break in toward the middle.
ORANGE WIDE: Count 5 steps upfield, then break in toward the middle.

ORANGE DART

Type: PASS (play-action) — looks exactly like ORANGE GHOST, then throws to the 5-step IN route. Installed LAST, only after the run game is reliable.
Ball carrier's real path Fake / decoy Pass path
CENTER: Hike the ball, then run five steps ahead and turn to help underneath.
QB: Run the ORANGE GHOST fake, then plant on step 2 and throw to ORANGE WIDE instead of handing off.
BLUE BACK: Run the identical empty-handed ORANGE GHOST sell — sprint like you have the ball.
ORANGE BACK: Sell the mesh, then release outside as the checkdown.
BLUE WIDE: Same 5-step IN every play — mirror target.
ORANGE WIDE: Same 5-step IN every play — primary target.

BLUE FLYER

Type: RUN — massive misdirection. Hardest play in the book. Installed LAST.
Ball carrier's real path Fake / decoy / motion
CENTER: Hike the ball, run 3 steps, turn around and wait — outlet only, never thrown the ball.
QB: Fake to Orange Back, roll right, hand off for real to Blue Back, keep rolling ball-less.
BLUE BACK: Take the real handoff and run the BLUE SWEEP path — you keep the ball and finish the play.
ORANGE BACK: Take the fake, then cut hard to the orange side like an orange sweep.
BLUE WIDE: Jet motion behind the line, pass BB's LEFT shoulder (no exchange), continue across to orange side, turn upfield.
ORANGE WIDE: Same 5-step IN every play — unchanged.

FORT (Man Coverage — Starting Defense)

Six defenders, six jobs. One blitzer, two hawks, two man corners, one deep safety — cover your person, not a box.
Offense (circle) Defense (square) Man coverage link
BLITZ: Start on the 7-yard mark and sprint straight at the QB on every snap.
BLUE HAWK: Stand your ground on the blue side — don't cross until the ball is thrown.
ORANGE HAWK: Stand your ground on the orange side — don't cross until the ball is thrown.
BLUE CB: Stick to your one receiver like glue.
ORANGE CB: Stick to your one receiver like glue.
SAFETY: Own the deep middle grass — nobody gets behind you.

FENCE (2-3-1 Zone — Alternate Defense)

Six defenders, six jobs. 2 line, 3 linebackers (MIKE blitzes), 1 deep safety — cover your box, not a person.
Offense (circle) Defense (square) Zone third
MIKE (blitzer): Start on the 7-yard mark and sprint straight at the QB on every snap.
BLUE LINE: Stand your ground on the blue side — don't cross until the ball is thrown.
ORANGE LINE: Stand your ground on the orange side — don't cross until the ball is thrown.
BLUE THIRD: This box is your grass — pull the flag of whoever comes into it.
ORANGE THIRD: This box is your grass — pull the flag of whoever comes into it.
SAFETY: Own the deep middle grass — nobody gets behind you.
